# DEC-02: Is the late fee capped?

## Question

Is the late fee capped?

## Why now

The fee rule is in the backlog and has no end today.

## Known and unknown

| Known | Unknown |
|---|---|
| 2 per day (PROVISIONAL) | What the board will vote |

## Options

### Option A: Cap the late fee at 10

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost | None |
| What it closes | The amount a member can owe for one tool |
| Risks | The board may want a higher cap |
| Calendar impact | No impact |

### Option B: No cap

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost | None |
| What it closes | The amount a member can owe for one tool |
| Risks | A forgotten tool owes more than it is worth |
| Calendar impact | No impact |

## Recommendation and its price

Cap the late fee at 10: a member never owes more than a cheap tool costs.

## What would change our mind

A board vote for another cap

## Repo changes per option

- Option A: docs/D1.md PAR-02 and docs/PLAN.md LOAN-03 carry the cap
- Option B: docs/D1.md PAR-02 carries no cap

## Decision

> [!DECISION]
> Decided: yes. Option: A (Cap the late fee at 10). By: owner. Date: 2026-11-18.
