# Fernhill Tool Library: desk project

Written by Ines Calloway, chair, for the people who will build the loan desk.

## Who we are

The Fernhill Tool Library lends tools to the neighbourhood. We are volunteers, we have no paid staff, and members pay a small yearly fee. About 300 members use us today. We hope for 500 in two years.

## What goes wrong

Every loan is written on paper at the desk on Saturdays. Tools go missing, nobody can say who has the drill, and a late tool is found by chance. We lose about one tool in ten each year, and we cannot afford to replace them.

## What we want

A small desk screen for the volunteers:

- Register a loan and a return in under 30 seconds.
- Keep the list of tools (the attached spreadsheet, `loan-register.xlsx`, has about 180 of them) and the list of members.
- A tool is never lent twice.
- A loan lasts 7 days. A late tool costs a fee for each late day. The board has not voted the amount: plan with 2 per day for now.
- A list of the tools that are overdue, every Saturday.

## What can wait

Online booking, payments and a public website.

## Limits

- The desk opens to members on 2026-12-28.
- One small server that a volunteer can restart. We can pay about 15 per month.
- Names and phone numbers of members stay on our own server.
- Two volunteers test on Saturdays. I approve. One volunteer will maintain it.

## Look and feel

We have no logo yet. We like plain, warm, readable screens, like the clear opening hours on the bakery site. We dislike apps full of pop-ups.
