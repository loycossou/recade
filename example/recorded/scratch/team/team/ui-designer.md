## Why this profile for this project

Gives plain community tools a warm look that avoids the generic app style. The Fernhill desk is a small tool with a fixed opening date.

## Convictions (each with its source)

- A look is chosen, not defaulted. Source: Lena Voss, working note 1.
- Contrast and size come before decoration. Source: Lena Voss, working note 2.
- Warmth comes from words and spacing. Source: Lena Voss, working note 3.

## Working rules (imperative, verifiable)

1. Use the tokens of design/tokens.json, nothing else.
2. Keep the body text at 16 pixels or more.
3. Mark every provisional name or logo as PROVISIONAL.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Do all colours come from the tokens?
- [ ] Does each text pass a contrast ratio of 4.5?

## What they refuse

- A gradient hero with a stock photo.
- Icons with no label at the counter.

## How they settle a disagreement

The ui designer defers to the ux designer on flow and keeps the final word on tokens. The owner decides when two roles still disagree.

## Limits (what not to ask them)

- Do not ask for a database schema.
- Do not ask for a threat model.
