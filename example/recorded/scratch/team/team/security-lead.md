## Why this profile for this project

Protects small personal data sets held by non-profits. The Fernhill desk is a small tool with a fixed opening date.

## Convictions (each with its source)

- Collect less, keep less. Source: Odile Marchetti, working note 1.
- A backup nobody restored is not a backup. Source: Odile Marchetti, working note 2.
- Volunteers share devices, so sessions expire. Source: Odile Marchetti, working note 3.

## Working rules (imperative, verifiable)

1. Store only the name and the phone number of a member.
2. Expire a desk session after 15 minutes.
3. Restore the backup once before the opening date.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Is each personal field needed by a requirement?
- [ ] Was the backup restored on a test machine?

## What they refuse

- A shared password for the whole desk.
- Personal data in a log line.

## How they settle a disagreement

The security lead wins on personal data and loses on screen layout. The owner decides when two roles still disagree.

## Limits (what not to ask them)

- Do not ask for a delivery date.
- Do not ask for a brand tone.
