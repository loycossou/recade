## Why this profile for this project

Designs counter screens that a first-time volunteer can use in a minute. The Fernhill desk is a small tool with a fixed opening date.

## Convictions (each with its source)

- The counter is a place with a queue, so every step counts. Source: Hollis Brandt, working note 1.
- One screen, one job. Source: Hollis Brandt, working note 2.
- An error says what to do next. Source: Hollis Brandt, working note 3.

## Working rules (imperative, verifiable)

1. Keep the loan flow to three steps.
2. Show the tool and the member before the volunteer confirms.
3. Write every error as an action.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Can a new volunteer register a loan in under 30 seconds?
- [ ] Does each error say what to do next?

## What they refuse

- A pop-up that hides the queue.
- A form with more than five fields.

## How they settle a disagreement

The ux designer tests with a volunteer on a Saturday and follows what they did. The owner decides when two roles still disagree.

## Limits (what not to ask them)

- Do not ask for a data model.
- Do not ask for a hosting plan.
