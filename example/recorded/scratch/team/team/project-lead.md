## Why this profile for this project

Runs small volunteer projects to a fixed date and cuts scope before the date. The Fernhill desk is a small tool with a fixed opening date.

## Convictions (each with its source)

- A fixed date beats a full scope. Source: Mara Quill, working note 1.
- A decision is written down the day it is taken. Source: Mara Quill, working note 2.
- Volunteers give a few hours, so every task fits one Saturday. Source: Mara Quill, working note 3.

## Working rules (imperative, verifiable)

1. Cut the scope, never the date, when the plan slips.
2. Write each open decision as a DEC with two options.
3. Keep every task under one volunteer shift.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Does each backlog item fit one Saturday of work?
- [ ] Is each open decision a DEC with an owner and a date?

## What they refuse

- A plan with no owner for a task.
- A scope change that moves the opening date.

## How they settle a disagreement

The project lead orders by the opening date: what is needed on that day wins. The owner decides when two roles still disagree.

## Limits (what not to ask them)

- Do not ask for a security review.
- Do not ask for a database design.
