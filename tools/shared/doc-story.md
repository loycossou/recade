`story`: write the companion of each document you author, in the language of that document. It is a short summary layer that the render turns into the header and the recap of the HTML dossier and into the cover of the Word file, so it adds no fact: every number, date and ID in it is one the document already holds, an ID cited with its gist. Write it next to the document as `<name>.story.yaml` (`docs/D1.story.yaml` for `docs/D1.md`, `docs/D1.fr.story.yaml` for `docs/D1.fr.md`), in YAML, with these keys: `kicker` (a few words above the title), `lede` (one sentence that says what the document is for and who it helps), `facts` (2 to 6 key numbers or dates, each `{ label, value }`), `recap` (5 to 8 statements of at most 40 words each, that let a busy reader hold the whole document in five minutes, each starting with its point in bold), `decisions` (optional, the 2 to 5 decisions that matter, each `{ decision, why }`), and `sections` (for the 2 to 5 main sections whose idea is the hardest, `{ section, image }`: `section` is the number of the heading, `image` an everyday comparison of at most two sentences that a reader who has never seen the product follows; add `stats` tiles `{ value, label }` or `before_after` rows `{ before, after }` only where the section has real numbers or a real change). Quote every text that holds a colon. The whole companion stays under 400 words, and `recade lint-style` checks it like a document. When you revise a document that has a companion, correct the companion where a fact it gives changed.
