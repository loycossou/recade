At most 10 findings, the blockers first. `blocker`: the document cannot be approved or built as written. `should fix`: a real defect. `nit`: taste, which is listed and never applied. Do not raise what `recade lint-style` checks. Raise a `should fix` when the document repeats itself: the same rule or decision restated in several sections, a table where a sentence would do, a section that restates another document.
